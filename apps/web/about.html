About

What this is, and what it is not

A free, open-source tool for reading Scripture and thinking carefully about it. Built to be honest about its own limits.

The short version

Great Church AI is a small web app. You can read the Bible in it, search all of it, study a passage, put together a prayer, and ask questions about Christian content. It is free, it has no account system, and the Scripture it serves is stored on the server rather than fetched from somewhere.

The parts of it that generate words use a large language model. A model is a statistical system that produces plausible text. It is not a person, and it is not a source of authority. It can be confidently wrong about a verse, about a doctrine, or about your situation. Everything below exists so you can calibrate how much weight to give it.

Which Bible is this?

The King James Version, 1611. It is in the public domain, so it can be shipped with the app and read without a network connection, an account, or a rate limit.

KJV was chosen because it is the oldest complete English translation still in wide use and because its being public domain makes an offline, free, no-tracking Bible reader possible at all. It is not the easiest translation to read, and if you would rather study a modern one, use this as a reference and read your preferred translation alongside it. Nothing on this site is a reason to stop using the Bible your church gave you.

Every passage is read from the text that ships with the server, so the reference and the words next to it always match. If a passage cannot be read, the app says so instead of filling the gap.

What it cannot do

This is the part worth reading.

It is not a pastor

It cannot baptise, marry you, bury someone, give you communion, absolve you, or walk with you through a crisis. Those belong to people who know you, and to God.

It cannot prophesy

It will not tell you what God has planned, what he has said to you, or what will happen next. Anyone claiming software can do that is either confused or lying to you.

It cannot settle an argument

Where Christians honestly disagree, it will usually present the competing readings rather than pick a winner. If it sounds certain about something genuinely disputed, do not trust it.

It is not a substitute for help

Not for medical, legal, financial, or mental-health matters, and not for abuse or safety situations. If you are in danger, contact your local emergency services. If you are struggling, tell someone who can actually sit with you.

Its quotes need checking

Scripture shown in the reader is the real stored text. Scripture quoted inside a generated answer comes from a model and can be misquoted or invented. Open any quoted verse in the reader to confirm it.

Its views are not your church's

It has no denomination, no creed, and no idea what your church teaches. When it offers an interpretation, it is offering a reading — not your tradition's settled position.

Your data

There is no account, no sign-up, and no analytics on this site. Nothing you type is written to a database by this app.

When you ask a question, this server works out what you are asking and assembles a prompt. The conversation is then handled by Puter, which runs the model in your browser. That means your question is sent to Puter rather than stored here, and Puter's own privacy terms apply to you. Prayers you save are kept in your browser's local storage and never leave your device.

The one exception is a deployment that deliberately configures a server-side AI provider. In that case prompts are sent to whoever is named in the server's environment configuration, and that operator's terms apply. This project ships with no such provider configured.

Open source

The whole thing — server, front end, and this page — is MIT licensed and free to read, copy, and change. If your church wants its own instance with its own name, its own colours, and no third-party involvement at all, that is a few commands away and you are welcome to do it. 

git clone https://github.com/Omonire/GREAT_CHURCH_AI
cd GREAT_CHURCH_AI
pip install -e ./apps/api
python -m church_ai_api

Then open http://127.0.0.1:8000. The Bible reader and search need no configuration and no API key at all. AI features need Puter, which asks the visitor to sign in the first time they use it.
