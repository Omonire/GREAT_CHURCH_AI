A study tool, not an oracle

Great Church AI is software for exploring Christian content. It is not God, a pastor, or a prophet, and it does not speak with divine authority. When Christians disagree about a passage, it shows you the competing readings rather than picking a winner.

Private by default

Your questions are not stored on our servers and there is no account to create. Scripture runs entirely from data bundled with the app, so reading and searching keep working with no connection and no third-party tracking.

How it works

Text first, commentary second

The most useful thing an AI can do with a difficult passage is stop pretending it is simple. Great Church AI keeps the biblical text in view and treats interpretation as interpretation.

1 Pick a book and chapter, or search for a word

2 Read the actual passage, verse by verse

3 Ask for an explanation, themes, or reflection questions

4 Turn it into a prayer when you are ready

What this is, and what it isn't

It will help with

  • Understanding what a passage is actually saying
  • Translating archaic or difficult wording into modern English
  • Tracing an idea through the rest of Scripture
  • Finding verses on a theme, with the references named
  • Separating what the text says from what people have argued about it
  • Giving you language to pray when you are stuck

It will not do

  • Speak as God, the Holy Spirit, a prophet, or a pastor
  • Settle a theological dispute your church is still having
  • Replace a pastor, counsellor, doctor, or lawyer
  • Invent a verse, or quote a translation it cannot verify
  • Tell you a passage was selected for you by God
  • Handle an emergency — please contact your church or local services
